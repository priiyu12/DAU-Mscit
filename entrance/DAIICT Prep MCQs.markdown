# DAIICT Entrance Exam Preparation MCQs

## Instructions
- **Topics**: Functions, Relations, Sets, Time & Work
- **Difficulty**: More difficult than easy
- **Scoring**: +4 for correct, -1 for incorrect
- **Options**: 4 per question, "None of These" (NOTA) where applicable
- **Answers**: Provided at the end for self-evaluation

### Questions

#### Sets
1. Let \( A = \{1, 3, 5, 7\} \), \( B = \{3, 4, 5, 6\} \), and \( C = \{5, 6, 7, 8\} \). What is \( (A \cap B) \cup (B \cap C) \)?  
   a) \{3, 5\}  
   b) \{3, 5, 6\}  
   c) \{3, 4, 5, 6\}  
   d) \{5, 6\}

2. The power set of a set \( S \) has 128 elements. How many elements are in \( S \)?  
   a) 6  
   b) 7  
   c) 8  
   d) 9

3. In a survey of 200 students, 120 like math, 90 like physics, and 70 like both. How many students like at least one subject?  
   a) 140  
   b) 150  
   c) 160  
   d) 170

4. If \( |A| = 4 \) and \( |B| = 3 \), what is the number of elements in \( A \times B \)?  
   a) 7  
   b) 12  
   c) 16  
   d) 10

5. How many subsets of \( \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\} \) contain exactly 3 elements?  
   a) 10  
   b) 15  
   c) 20  
   d) 25

6. If \( A \subseteq B \) and \( |A| = 5 \), \( |B| = 7 \), how many possible sets \( A \) exist?  
   a) 32  
   b) 64  
   c) 21  
   d) 35

7. For sets \( A \) and \( B \), if \( A \cup B = A \cap B \), then:  
   a) \( A \subseteq B \)  
   b) \( B \subseteq A \)  
   c) \( A = B \)  
   d) None of These

8. In a group of 150 people, 80 like tea, 70 like coffee, and 40 like neither. How many like both?  
   a) 30  
   b) 40  
   c) 50  
   d) 60

9. If \( P(A) \) denotes the power set of \( A \), and \( |P(A)| = 16 \), how many proper subsets does \( A \) have?  
   a) 15  
   b) 16  
   c) 14  
   d) 12

10. Let \( U = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\} \), \( A = \{1, 2, 3\} \), \( B = \{2, 4, 6\} \). What is \( (A' \cap B')' \)?  
    a) \{2\}  
    b) \{1, 4, 6\}  
    c) \{2, 4, 6\}  
    d) \{1, 2, 3, 4, 6\}

#### Relations
11. Which relation on \( \{1, 2, 3\} \) is reflexive and symmetric but not transitive?  
    a) \( \{(1,1), (2,2), (3,3), (1,2), (2,1)\} \)  
    b) \( \{(1,1), (2,2), (3,3), (2,3), (3,2)\} \)  
    c) \( \{(1,1), (2,2), (1,2), (2,1), (2,3), (3,2)\} \)  
    d) None of These

12. A relation \( R \) on integers is defined by \( a R b \) if \( a^2 = b^2 \). Is \( R \) an equivalence relation?  
    a) Yes  
    b) No  
    c) Only reflexive  
    d) Only symmetric

13. How many symmetric relations are possible on a set with 3 elements?  
    a) 64  
    b) 32  
    c) 16  
    d) 8

14. A relation \( R \) on \( \{1, 2, 3, 4\} \) has 10 ordered pairs. What is the minimum number of pairs to add to make it reflexive?  
    a) 0  
    b) 1  
    c) 2  
    d) 4

15. If \( R \) is a transitive relation on a set \( A \) with \( |A| = 3 \), what is the maximum number of ordered pairs \( R \) can have?  
    a) 6  
    b) 9  
    c) 12  
    d) 15

16. Define \( R \) on \( \mathbb{Z} \) by \( a R b \) if \( |a - b| \leq 2 \). Is \( R \) transitive?  
    a) Yes  
    b) No  
    c) Only reflexive  
    d) Only symmetric

17. How many equivalence relations are there on a set with 2 elements?  
    a) 1  
    b) 2  
    c) 3  
    d) 4

18. A relation \( R \) on \( \{a, b, c\} \) is symmetric and has exactly 4 ordered pairs. Which could be \( R \)?  
    a) \( \{(a,a), (b,b), (c,c), (a,b)\} \)  
    b) \( \{(a,b), (b,a), (b,c), (c,b)\} \)  
    c) \( \{(a,a), (b,b), (a,c), (c,a)\} \)  
    d) None of These

19. If \( R \) and \( S \) are equivalence relations on a set \( A \), is \( R \cap S \) always an equivalence relation?  
    a) Yes  
    b) No  
    c) Only reflexive  
    d) Only transitive

20. How many reflexive relations exist on a set with 4 elements?  
    a) \( 2^{12} \)  
    b) \( 2^{16} \)  
    c) \( 2^{10} \)  
    d) \( 2^{8} \)

#### Functions
21. Let \( f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R} \) be defined by \( f(x) = x^3 - x \). Is \( f \) injective?  
    a) Yes  
    b) No  
    c) Only for \( x \geq 0 \)  
    d) Only for \( x \leq 0 \)

22. A function \( f: \{1, 2, 3\} \to \{a, b\} \) is defined by \( f(1) = a \), \( f(2) = b \), \( f(3) = a \). Is \( f \) surjective?  
    a) Yes  
    b) No  
    c) Only injective  
    d) None of These

23. If \( f: A \to B \) and \( g: B \to C \) are both bijective, then \( g \circ f \) is:  
    a) Injective only  
    b) Surjective only  
    c) Bijective  
    d) Neither

24. How many injective functions are there from \( \{1, 2\} \) to \( \{1, 2, 3, 4\} \)?  
    a) 12  
    b) 16  
    c) 8  
    d) 6

25. Let \( f(x) = 2x + 1 \) and \( g(x) = x^2 \). What is \( f(g(-1)) \)?  
    a) 1  
    b) 3  
    c) 5  
    d) 7

26. If \( f: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R} \) is defined by \( f(x) = |x| \), how many solutions does \( f(x) = 2 \) have?  
    a) 1  
    b) 2  
    c) 3  
    d) None of These

27. A function \( f \) satisfies \( f(f(x)) = x \) for all \( x \) in its domain. \( f \) is:  
    a) Injective only  
    b) Surjective only  
    c) Bijective  
    d) None of These

28. How many surjective functions exist from \( \{1, 2, 3\} \) to \( \{a, b\} \)?  
    a) 4  
    b) 6  
    c) 8  
    d) 10

29. If \( f(x) = x^2 \) and \( g(x) = \sqrt{x} \) (for \( x \geq 0 \)), what is \( g(f(2)) \)?  
    a) 2  
    b) 4  
    c) 6  
    d) 8

30. Let \( f: \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z} \) be defined by \( f(n) = n + 1 \). Is \( f \) bijective?  
    a) Yes  
    b) No  
    c) Only injective  
    d) Only surjective

#### Time & Work
31. A can do a work in 12 days, B in 18 days. Together, they work for 4 days, then A leaves. How many more days does B need?  
    a) 8  
    b) 10  
    c) 12  
    d) 14

32. If 6 men can build a wall in 8 days, how many days will 4 men take?  
    a) 10  
    b) 12  
    c) 14  
    d) 16

33. A pipe fills a tank in 5 hours, and a leak empties it in 10 hours. Together, how long to fill the tank?  
    a) 6 hours  
    b) 8 hours  
    c) 10 hours  
    d) 12 hours

34. A and B together can do a work in 6 days. If A is twice as efficient as B, how long does A alone take?  
    a) 8 days  
    b) 9 days  
    c) 10 days  
    d) 12 days

35. If 5 workers finish a job in 20 days, how many workers are needed to finish it in 10 days?  
    a) 8  
    b) 10  
    c) 12  
    d) 15

36. A can do a work in 15 days. After 5 days, B joins, and they finish in 5 more days. How long does B alone take?  
    a) 10 days  
    b) 12 days  
    c) 15 days  
    d) 20 days

37. Two pipes A and B fill a tank in 12 and 15 hours, respectively. If both are opened alternately for 1 hour each, starting with A, how long to fill?  
    a) 13 hours  
    b) 14 hours  
    c) 15 hours  
    d) 16 hours

38. If 3 men and 4 boys can do a work in 8 days, while 2 men and 3 boys take 10 days, how long for 1 man alone?  
    a) 20 days  
    b) 24 days  
    c) 30 days  
    d) 36 days

39. A works twice as fast as B. Together, they finish a job in 6 days. How long for B alone?  
    a) 12 days  
    b) 15 days  
    c) 18 days  
    d) 21 days

40. A tank is filled by two pipes in 20 and 30 minutes. If both run together, how long to fill?  
    a) 12 minutes  
    b) 15 minutes  
    c) 18 minutes  
    d) 20 minutes
